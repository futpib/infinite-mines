FIELD MANUAL

Keep moving

Reveal

Guarded desktop controls reveal concealed tiles with Ctrl + click or double-click. On touch, hold then release; opened clues tap normally.

Mark

Right-click or Shift-click on desktop. A short touch cycles flag, question, clear; the visible Reveal tool is an explicit tap-to-reveal override.

Chord

Click an open number when its mines are flagged to clear the rest.

Survive

A blast costs one life. Score milestones earn lives back.

Explore

Drag the field in any direction. It is generated only as you need it.

Topology

Square clues count 8 touching cells, triangular clues count 12, and rhombille clues count 10.

Collect

Rare cyan artifacts are safe. Reveal them to add to Things.

Reference

Hover a cell, then click its coordinate card to copy a reproducible debug reference.
